# Solas 1.0.1

## 🐞 Bug fixes

- Pause when using other apps now rests a screen only while another app's window covers most of it. Dragging desktop icons no longer freezes the pond, and small windows, tooltips, and Finder's desktop leave the water running.
- Click ripples no longer go silent until relaunch after a sleep/wake, display wake, or user-session switch; Solas re-registers its click monitors on those transitions and refreshes them periodically, so a silent failure recovers within seconds instead of waiting for a relaunch.
- A right-click menu can no longer swallow a later desktop click: the next left click resolves the suppression even when it lands on a menu item or another app.
