# Solas 1.1.0

## ✨ What's new

- A new welcome experience introduces your pond, ripples, fish feeding, and essential settings, with an option to activate a license or enjoy the free trial. Revisit it anytime from the menu.
- Jade Depths brings a shaded slate-and-teal riverbed and soft sunlight to your pond.
- Follow Device Time now offers a custom daily schedule. Choose when each weather begins, or keep Auto for the familiar day-to-night cycle.
- Weather choices are easier to browse, grouped into Time of Day, Water & Depth, and Rain & Storm.
- Refreshed daylight, sunset, and nighttime lighting gives each time of day a more distinct atmosphere.
- Refined water currents have softer broad bands and clearer fine detail that gently fades toward the pond’s center. Highlights complement your water color and the weather’s light.
- Reset All Settings in General restores your preferences to their defaults while keeping your license and trial.

## 🐞 Bug fixes

- Clarity now follows your slider setting consistently across all weather presets.
- Current Strength now works across its full range, including values above 100%.
- Settings opens on your current desktop. Closing the last Solas window removes its Dock icon while the pond keeps running.
