# Solas 1.0

First release.

## ✨ What's new

- Living pond wallpaper: sits above your wallpaper, below your icons.
- Weather presets plus Follow Device Time, from dawn to moonlight.
- Water color, clarity, touch ripples, currents with strength, speed, and core glow.
- Fish with behavior modes plus feed-the-fish (hold and click to sprinkle food), active koi counts.
- Decorations: lily pads, flower styles and counts.
- Motion speed, performance controls (pause on battery, pause when using apps, frame rates, Pixel/Balanced/Smooth quality), launch at login.
- 3-day free trial, then one license covers up to 3 Macs.
- Automatic updates via Sparkle with About-pane toggles.
