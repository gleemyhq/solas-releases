# Solas 1.0.3

## ✨ What's new

- The pond now stays visible as a still wallpaper when your trial ends. Activate a license to resume animation.
- Koi are spaced apart in the still wallpaper shown after the trial ends.

## 🐞 Bug fixes

- Fixed app updates failing with "An error occurred while launching the installer."
- Fixed the wallpaper failing to appear or appearing late when launching with other apps open or switching desktops.
- Fixed enabled plants, including lily pads, missing from paused ponds.
- Fixed small fish disappearing when many koi were enabled.
